The Project

Project Overview (Alternate pitch approved by teacher)

This project involves a team of students collaborating, or an individual working towards creating a 3D space station scene using 3D modelling software. The scene will consist of a modelled space station, a backscene, and a short animation with each student responsible for designing and modelling the assets for the scene.

The goal of this assessment is to develop and showcase technological modelling skills, focusing on the understanding and application of various modelling types and tools within an appropriate 3D software. The object purpose is to fit well in a game design aspect.


Project Objectives:

  • apply and demonstrate knowledge of different technological modelling types and techniques.
  • evidence the trial and development stages of 3D modelling.
  • make informed decisions based on the use of models and to reflect on these decisions.
  • identify and manage potential problems, issues during the modelling process.
